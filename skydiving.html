What to Expect on Your Jump Day

Here is how your tandem skydiving experience unfolds from check-in to landing.

01. Check-In & Suit Up

Arrive at the VIP dropzone. Complete safety waivers, meet your dedicated tandem master, and get fitted with a precision harness.

02. Scenic Ascent

Enjoy a 15-minute panoramic flight in our turbine aircraft climbing up to 15,000 feet above the Arabian Gulf and dunes.

03. The 200 km/h Leap

The aircraft door opens. Step into the sky for 60 seconds of pure, unrestrained adrenaline freefall over the desert coast!

04. Canopy & 4K Video

Your parachute opens at 5,000 ft for a peaceful 5-minute canopy glide before a gentle landing and receiving your edited 4K video.
