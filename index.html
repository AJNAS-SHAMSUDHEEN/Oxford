Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Inland Sea Desert Safari?
Includes private pick-up from anywhere in Doha in a luxury Land Cruiser, professional desert marshal, dune bashing, sandboarding, camel rides, and traditional Arabian tea or coffee.
Is prior training required for 15,000 ft tandem skydiving?
No prior experience is necessary. You will jump harnessed to a certified USPA tandem master after a straightforward 20-minute safety briefing.
What is the best season to explore Qatar?
October through April is ideal, with pleasant temperatures (20°C – 28°C), crystal clear skies, and calm sea conditions for water sports.
