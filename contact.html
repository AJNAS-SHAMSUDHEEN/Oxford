Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Inland Sea Safari?

Private roundtrip pickup from any Doha hotel or airport in an executive Toyota Land Cruiser V8, professional desert marshal, 50m dune bashing, sandboarding, camel rides, and traditional Arabic hospitality at our beach camp.

Is prior experience needed for skydiving?

None whatsoever. You jump securely harnessed to a certified USPA tandem master instructor after an informative 20-minute ground briefing.

What should I wear on desert and boat tours?

Comfortable, breathable cotton attire and sunglasses. For yacht charters, swimwear and boat-friendly footwear are recommended. In desert winter evenings, bring a light jacket.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer full refunds or flexible rescheduling up to 24 hours prior to departure. Flights or marine departures impacted by severe weather conditions are fully refunded or rebooked.
