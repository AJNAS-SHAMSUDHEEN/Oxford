❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

What is included in the Inland Sea Desert Safari?

All our desert safaris include private pick-up from any hotel or residence in Doha in a luxury Toyota Land Cruiser, licensed desert safari captain, dune bashing, sandboarding, camel rides, bottled water, and traditional Arabic tea or coffee.

Are tandem skydiving jumps safe for first-time adventurers?

Absolutely! You will be securely harnessed to a certified USPA tandem master with thousands of jumps of experience. Prior training is not required—a 20-minute pre-flight briefing covers everything you need to know.

What should I wear during desert or mangrove tours?

For desert safaris, comfortable lightweight clothing, sunglasses, and sandals or trainers are recommended. For kayaking at Purple Island, wear swimwear or quick-dry activewear and water shoes.

What is your cancellation and weather rescheduling policy?

We offer 100% free cancellation or date rescheduling up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled expedition. In the unlikely event of extreme weather or high winds, you will receive an immediate reschedule or full refund.
