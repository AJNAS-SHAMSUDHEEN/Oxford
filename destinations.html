🌤 Best Season to Visit Qatar

Qatar enjoys pleasant, crisp outdoor weather between October and April, with average daytime temperatures between 20°C and 28°C (68°F – 82°F) — ideal for desert safaris, skydiving, overnight camping, and mangrove kayaking.

Oct – Dec (Golden Season)
Crisp breezes, perfect for desert safaris, skydiving, and luxury yachting.
Jan – Mar (Peak Camping)
Cool evenings, stargazing, falconry festivals, and mangrove flamingo season.
Apr – May (Marine Thrills)
Warm ocean waters, coral reef scuba diving, wakeboarding, and jet skiing.
