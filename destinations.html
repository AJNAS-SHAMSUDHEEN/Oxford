✦ Seasonal Travel Intelligence

Best Time to Visit Qatar

Qatar boasts glorious sunshine year-round. For desert safaris, skydiving, and outdoor adventures, the prime season is October through April when daytime temperatures average a pleasant 22°C – 28°C.

  • Oct – Apr: Perfect safari & skydiving weather with gentle sea breezes.
  • May – Sep: Ideal for luxury air-conditioned yachting, sunset cruises & night safaris.
